Guide to all ten products

Where is my information saved?

If cloud storage is configured, the app uses a private workspace tied to a signed browser cookie. Otherwise the app clearly shows demo mode and saves records only on this browser. The overview gathers available records from both modes. Clearing browser data may remove access; export a copy first.

Are these real bookings or payments?

No. Listings and prices are sample data. Requests are saved for testing but are not sent to providers; no booking, availability, payment, or deposit is confirmed. ShopPadi records are a personal ledger, not a bank connection.

Can I see records from the ten separate sites?

Those sites live on other domains, so their browser sessions and local records do not automatically move into OnePadi. New records you create within this ecosystem appear here.
